Setup: J feasible (each s in S has exactly 2 incidences; each H-vertex h has

Proof. Take any feasible J' with a not in J'. Let D = J symmetric-diff J' (on
incidence edges); call a D-edge OLD if in J\J', NEW if in J'\J. a is OLD. At every

the deficient s; take a NEW edge (s,h1) [J' selects an incidence at s other than
a], then if h1 is saturated in J-a take its OLD edge to some s1, then a NEW edge

degree 2, t gains one unit (still <= cap), all interior degrees unchanged, a not
re-added. Result feasible and avoids a. QED.
